# Project artwork

Created using the built-in Imagegen tool, not the CLI. Assets saved locally as `assets/hands.png` and `assets/animals.png`. Each animal and hand is rendered independently by `art.js` using an explicit SVG clip path around its source pixels. The viewBox fits that region, and the clip path prevents adjacent artwork from showing in letterboxing at different destination ratios. The small stepped border between rabbit and cat preserves their whiskers and tail. Source PNGs are reused without altering the illustrations.

## Hands prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset: preschool counting hand photographic sprite sheet. Create one wide landscape image on a pure white background, exactly FIVE equal-width columns, a single row. Each column contains ONE isolated photorealistic right human hand and wrist palm facing camera, same natural warm skin, clean anatomy. Left to right: 1) index finger extended vertically, thumb and other three fingers folded into palm. 2) index and middle extended V, thumb holding ring and pinky folded. 3) index middle ring extended, thumb holding pinky folded. 4) index middle ring pinky extended, thumb folded clearly across palm. 5) all five digits extended, thumb clearly to side. Exactly five anatomically accurate digits per hand total. Each hand centered fully within its column, equal scale and baseline, ample white gutters between. Soft photographic studio lighting, realistic skin texture, no cartoon styling, no jewelry, no text, no border, no extra items. Suitable as clear educational reference of finger counts 1 through 5.

## Animals prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: adorable preschool browser game animal sprite sheet. Wide landscape image, pure white background. Exactly FIVE equal-width columns in ONE horizontal row, each containing one full isolated character, equal-sized and centered with generous clear white gutters. Left to right: friendly brown mosquito with blue translucent wings and a long proboscis, cute white rabbit with pink ears, cheerful orange kitten, smiling coral red crab with two claws, beautiful yellow and pink butterfly with symmetrical open wings. Style: premium hand-painted children's picture book, soft gouache watercolor textures, warm outlines, cheerful faces, rosy cheeks, tasteful soft pastel colors, consistent friendly style, sophisticated sweet illustration, not emoji, not 3D. Each character entirely fits inside its own column. No text, no letters, no numbers, no accessories, no scenery, no shadows outside the characters.
